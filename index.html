About Tethes (UST)

TRON Tethes (UST) is engineered on the robust TRON blockchain, ensuring lightning-fast transaction confirmation speeds and minimal network fees. Designed as a competitive stable medium of exchange, UST brings reliability and scalability to everyday digital transactions and the decentralized finance (DeFi) ecosystem.


Our smart contract infrastructure utilizes an advanced Upgradeable Proxy Architecture (TethesProxy.sol combined with TethesTokenv1.sol). This ensures that the token's core logic can be upgraded in the future without ever risking user balances, preserving the complete state and security of the network.
